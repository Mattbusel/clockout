Clockout is a free tip and shift tracker for servers, bartenders, baristas, delivery drivers, stylists and anyone paid hourly plus tips.

Punch out, type the night in twenty seconds, go home. Wages plus tips, minus what you tipped out, divided by the hours you actually worked: that is your real hourly rate, and Clockout shows it for every shift, every week and every job.

FREE, FOREVER
• Log unlimited shifts for your job, with its wage and tip-out rule
• Real hourly, take-home, the monthly goal and twelve weeks of tips and wages

PUNCH OUT
• Clock in and out (past midnight is handled) or just type the hours
• Sales, cash tips, card tips, and a tip-out worked out from your job's rule
• Live take-home and per-hour as you type

TONIGHT
• The month against your goal, with a pace line
• This week, this year, average per shift, tips as a share of sales
• Tonight's shift printed on receipt paper

SHIFTS
• Every shift by week, filtered by job or month, swipe to edit or delete
