# App Review notes

---

No account, login or network connection is required.

HOW TO USE: Tap the gear on the home screen and add a job (name, hourly wage, tip-out rule). Tap the amber plus button after a shift, choose the shift type, set clock in and out (or type hours), enter sales and tips, tap Punch out. The Shifts tab lists every shift (tap to edit, swipe to delete); Insights shows averages by weekday and shift type; Money has the paycheck check and the tax-year totals with a CSV export.

IN-APP PURCHASE: the app is free. One non-consumable, "Clockout Pro" (com.mattbusel.clockout.pro), unlocks the Insights tab, the Money tab (paycheck check, tax year, CSV export) and adding a second job. Logging shifts for one job is free forever. To see the paywall: tap the Insights or Money tab and tap "See Clockout Pro", or tap the gear on the home screen and tap SEE on the Clockout Pro card (or "Add another job (Pro)" once one job exists). Restore purchase is on the paywall and under the SEE button on the Clockout Pro card in Jobs & settings. No subscription, no ads.

PRIVACY: no data is collected. Everything is stored in a JSON file in the app's Documents folder on the device. There are no notifications, no network requests and no third-party code.

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Clockout is a personal income log for tipped workers (servers, bartenders, baristas, drivers). It records shifts and tips and shows the real hourly rate, weekly and yearly totals, a paycheck reconciliation and a year summary. General audience; rated 4+.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS
No setup, login or credentials. A new install starts empty; add one job in settings and log one shift.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS
None, apart from Apple's StoreKit for the one in-app purchase. No other network requests, analytics, advertising or third-party frameworks. Built with SwiftUI, Swift Charts and Foundation. The CSV export uses the system share sheet.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The currency symbol is a display setting chosen by the user.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / PROTECTED MATERIAL
Not applicable. The app records what the user types and does not give tax advice. All art, text and code are my own work.
